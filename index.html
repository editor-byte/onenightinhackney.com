    What does ‘accredited’ mean?

    Hackney Nights is Hackney Council’s night-time safety scheme. Accredited venues have signed up to its standards, including:

    • staff trained in customer welfare, spotting vulnerability and drink spiking
    • ‘Ask for Angela’: if you feel unsafe, ask the bar staff for ‘Angela’ and they will help you discreetly
    • zero tolerance of harassment, violence against women and hate

    It’s a council safety standard, not a review. Venues without it aren’t necessarily unsafe.
